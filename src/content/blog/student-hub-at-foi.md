The **Faculty of Organization and Informatics** opened Student Hub — a space in
the basement of the FOI 1 building dedicated to the work of student associations
and students generally.

The project was funded through the University of Zagreb and Student Centre
Varaždin, with the support of the faculty. The space itself was designed and
furnished by the students who use it.

## Why this mattered

During my time on the **Student Assembly** (February 2023 – February 2025) the
recurring problem was not enthusiasm, it was somewhere to put it. Associations
were organising events out of borrowed rooms and message threads.

A permanent room changes the economics of that. Meetings stop needing to be
scheduled around room availability. Equipment stays where it is used. Groups run
into each other and end up collaborating on things nobody planned.

It is a small piece of infrastructure and it removed a disproportionate amount of
friction — which is usually how infrastructure works.
